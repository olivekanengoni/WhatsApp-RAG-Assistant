# Sample Properties LLC - Tenant FAQ (sample document, fictional company)

## Security deposit
The security deposit is 5% of the annual rent for unfurnished apartments and 10% of the annual rent for furnished apartments. The deposit is refunded within 30 days of handover, minus any documented damage beyond normal wear and tear.

## Rent payment
Rent can be paid in up to four cheques per year or by bank transfer. Late payments incur an administration fee of AED 200.

## Renewal and termination
Tenants must give 90 days' written notice of non-renewal. Early termination before the end of the contract carries a penalty of two months' rent.

## Pets
Pets are allowed only with written approval from the landlord and a refundable pet deposit of AED 1,000. Dogs over 20 kg are not permitted.

## Maintenance
Emergency issues such as water leaks or no power are attended to within 4 hours. Non-urgent requests are handled within 3 working days. Tenants can report issues via WhatsApp or the tenant portal.

## Move-out
Book the move-out inspection at least 7 days before handover. Utility accounts must be closed and final bills provided at the inspection.
